### What this account is

This is the **build lab**, not the shop window. Everything I actually ship ends up here, in separate open repositories you can clone, run, and judge for yourself. My portfolio tells the story, LinkedIn tells the timeline, and this profile shows the work with nothing hidden behind a slide deck.

Short version of who I am: **Fullstack Machine Learning Engineer**, Software Engineering Technology (TRPL) graduate from Universitas Gadjah Mada, based in Yogyakarta. I build the model, the pipeline, and the interface in front of it. Recent work leans on computer vision, OCR, RAG, LLM integration, and the plumbing that keeps them alive in production.

<br />

I started in software engineering, not machine learning, which is why most of my ML work ships as a full product instead of a notebook. During my internship at PT Parama Data Unit I built a desktop computer vision system that estimates rock cuttings on shale shakers from CCTV footage, wired into the drilling floor's WITS Level 0 data, and made it run from Windows 7 to 11. In the contract year that followed I rewrote it for the web. For my thesis I put MobileViT and BiSeNet v2 behind a Next.js dashboard and served them through ONNX Runtime so the whole thing could live on a 2 vCPU / 2 GB VPS without a GPU.

At Advisor Lauren Co., Ltd (South Korea, remote) I co-led dataset construction for a vision-language model that detects defects on wind turbine blades: 100,000 real drone-inspection photos, 14 defect classes, 5 mentors and 35 annotators across Indonesia, with daily QC and guidelines I wrote myself.

Along the way: a UAV ground control station with GAMAFORCE UGM that helped take overall champion at KRTI 2025, an IoT sleep apnea monitor that placed 2nd at Hackathon elevAIte 2025, and a waste management app that reached the global top 100 of the GDSC Solution Challenge. Two papers from the shale shaker segmentation benchmark are under review.

These days I mostly build document intelligence: OCR pipelines that emit structured, confidence-scored JSON, and RAG assistants that cite their sources and refuse to answer when the context runs out.

### How I work

- **Ship the demo, not the diagram.** Every project above has a runnable path, and most have a live URL, because a model nobody can open is a model nobody will trust.
- **CPU-first.** ShaleShakerCuttingMonitor targets a 2 vCPU / 2 GB box with no GPU, which is why it runs on ONNX Runtime instead of PyTorch. Constraint-driven engineering keeps deployments cheap and boring.
- **Privacy as a feature.** PasFotoIn and Lolos-CV process everything on the user's device. No upload, no account, no server to leak.
- **Fail loudly.** The RAG assistant refuses when the context is thin and labels general-knowledge answers instead of silently blending them with source citations.
- **README first.** If a repository cannot be cloned and understood without calling me, the README is not finished.
